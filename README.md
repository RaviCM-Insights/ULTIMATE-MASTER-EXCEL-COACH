# Ultimate Master Excel Coach

Ultimate Master Excel Coach is a professional AI-driven Excel training framework designed to transform beginners into real-world corporate Excel professionals.

This project is not just focused on formulas. It teaches complete spreadsheet engineering, analytical thinking, business reporting, dashboard workflows, debugging systems, data cleaning, automation logic, and professional Excel problem-solving.

The system is structured like a real senior corporate mentor and focuses on practical employable skills used in modern business environments.

---

## Core Features

- Step-by-step Excel training
- Beginner to advanced progression system
- Real-world corporate business scenarios
- Formula breakdowns and debugging
- Dashboard and KPI training
- Data cleaning workflows
- Spreadsheet optimization methods
- Analytical thinking development
- Professional reporting systems
- Interview and challenge modes
- Practice datasets and exercises
- Excel engineering mindset training
